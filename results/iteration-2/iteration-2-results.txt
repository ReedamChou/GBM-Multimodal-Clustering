Step 7 did not create new clusters on the test set. It took the clusters learned on the train set in Step 5, used their centroids, and assigned each test patient to the nearest train-defined cluster in the same feature space. Then it asked three validation questions:

1. Do the test patients fall into roughly the same cluster proportions as train?
2. Does the “high-risk” cluster keep a similar biological/imaging signature on test?
3. Do survival differences across clusters still appear on test?

These results come from the leakage-safe rerun, where Step 3 no longer learned imputations or scaling on the full cohort. All learned preprocessing was fit on train only in Step 4 and then applied unchanged to test.

This changed the downstream clustering solutions and produced cleaner validation behavior than iteration 1.

UCSF is now a strong internal validation result.
